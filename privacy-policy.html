2. Scope

This policy applies to all Chrome extensions published by Nicolas Follezou on the Chrome Web Store, including TabZero, EasyRec, ClipStack and Kitty Lock. Each extension is described separately where their data practices differ.

3. Data We Collect — and What We Do Not

3.1 TabZero (Tab Workflows & Session Saver)

All your workflows (tab names, URLs, titles, favicons) and preferences are stored exclusively on your device using chrome.storage.local and never transmitted to any server. We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII).

TabZero transmits anonymous, non-identifiable usage events (e.g. "workflow saved", "workflow restored") to Plausible Analytics, a privacy-first analytics platform based in the European Union (Germany). Plausible uses no cookies, no localStorage, and stores no personally identifiable information. IP addresses are hashed and rotated every 24 hours solely to prevent spam, and are never persisted nor used to track individuals. The sole purpose is to understand feature usage and improve the extension.

3.2 EasyRec (Screen & Webcam Recorder)

EasyRec stores recording preferences locally on your device via chrome.storage.local. Recordings (video, screen captures, photos) are processed entirely in your browser and saved to your device through your browser's normal download. No recording content is ever uploaded to any server.

EasyRec transmits anonymous, non-identifiable usage events (e.g. "recording started", "video exported") to Plausible Analytics, a privacy-first analytics platform based in the European Union (Germany). Plausible uses no cookies, no localStorage, and stores no personally identifiable information. IP addresses are hashed and rotated every 24 hours solely to prevent spam, and are never persisted nor used to track individuals. The sole purpose is to understand feature usage and improve the extension.

3.3 ClipStack (Clipboard Manager & FIFO Queue)

We do not collect any personal data. ClipStack captures the text you explicitly copy (e.g., via Ctrl+C) to build your clipboard history. All data is stored exclusively on your device using chrome.storage.local. No clipboard data is ever transmitted to our servers or any third party.

3.4 Kitty Lock (Social Media Blocker)

We do not collect any personal data. Kitty Lock stores your settings (time slots, blocked sites), your virtual cat and its "graveyard" exclusively on your device using chrome.storage.local. To block a site, the extension only compares the domain of the page being opened with your block list; it never reads page content, never records your browsing history and sends nothing to our servers. Kitty Lock uses no analytics. The only exception is an optional Pro purchase, handled as described in section 5.

3.5 Our website (nicompc.github.io)

This site, including the page that may open when you install or uninstall one of the extensions, is a static page hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts. The optional ?from= and ?event= parameters in its address are only read by your browser to adapt the message; they are not stored by us. GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address for security purposes (GitHub Privacy Statement).
